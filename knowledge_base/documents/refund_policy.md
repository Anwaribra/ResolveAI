# Official Refund Policy

## Overview
We offer a full **30-day money-back guarantee** for all software subscription products.

## Eligibility Conditions
- Refund request must be submitted within 30 days of initial purchase date.
- Enterprise custom contract plans are non-refundable unless specified in the SLA.
- Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
