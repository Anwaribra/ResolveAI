# Account Locked Troubleshooting Policy

## Symptoms
User receives error: "Account locked due to multiple failed login attempts."

## Policy & Solution
1. Accounts are automatically locked for security after **5 consecutive failed password attempts**.
2. Lock duration is 30 minutes by default.
3. Users can immediately unlock their account by navigating to `https://app.example.com/unlock` and requesting a magic link via their registered email.
4. If an account remains locked after 30 minutes, users should reset their password.
